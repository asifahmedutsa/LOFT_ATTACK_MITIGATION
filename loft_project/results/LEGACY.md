# Legacy results — do not cite

The PNG/CSV files at the top level of this directory, `paper_results_section.md`, and
the scripts `generate_results.py` / `generate_final_results.py` all come from the
original prototype. Do not cite them, for three reasons:

- **Synthetic data with shortcuts.** They were evaluated on `data/full_dataset.csv`,
  where attack and benign rows are generated by different formulas. Single features
  reach AUC 0.80.
- **No real federation.** The "federated" results never applied aggregated weights.
- **Hard-coded numbers.** `generate_final_results.py` contains hard-coded values.

The audited reproduction of the 98.375 % smoke-test accuracy (dataset SHA-256
`632a44eb…05dff6`) is documented in `docs/novelty_feasibility_and_research_next_steps.md`.

Current evidence is in `results/v2/` and `docs/results_v2.md`.
