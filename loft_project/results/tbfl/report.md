## Choice (rule fixed before recording)

Eligible (T1B false alarms at most 0.2 %): one-shot, bagging, FedProx. The lossless model is not
eligible: 0.72 % false alarms on T1B at the threshold set on T1A. Best eligible score 37.93
(bagging); one-shot (37.87) is within 1 point and shares least. **FEVER's detector: one-shot
sharing (CatBoost), trained on testbed rows.** H1 (lossless at least 2 points above bagging) holds:
55.03 against 37.93.

## Reading

1. On testbed rows, as in the simulator, the standard attacks (G1-G3, Spoof, Pulse) are caught
   by every detector (99.7-100 %, FedProx 89-99 %), and sharing finished models (one-shot,
   bagging) does not carry Mimic from univ2 to bigFlows (0.05-0.10 %).
2. Training that combines the controllers' statistics (lossless) or data (pooled) carries it in
   part: Mimic 61-65 % (simulator rows: 97 %), Aware 23-28 % (simulator rows: 2-3 %).
3. Those models' threshold does not transfer from T1A to T1B: 0.72-0.88 % false alarms against a
   0.1 % budget, so the rule fixed in advance keeps one-shot sharing.
4. On the testbed, Mimic and Aware are stopped by the volume rule, which does not use the
   detector (v6 campaign); the detector's job there is the standard attacks.

The held-out univ2 test (UH) was not recorded. The baselines are not yet retrained on these rows.

## Amendment 1 (after these results, before the deployment check)

The supervisor requires the deployed detector to come from round-based federated learning, so
one-shot sharing is no longer deployed; FedAvg was added as an offline reference (above). The
deployment check runs Flower XGBoost bagging (fever-fl-bag, threshold 0.7376) and lossless federated
XGBoost (fever-fl-ll, threshold 0.4953) against v6 on the testbed; the rule that picks between them
is in docs/testbed_training_plan.md. Both are exported for the controller as TreeJSON
(loftbench/tbfl_export.py, export.json: largest difference from XGBoost 5e-7; 300 trees each, 0.23 s
to score a full 3,500-entry table in the testbed's Python, against 0.09 s for v6).
